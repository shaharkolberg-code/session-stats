Your chats, sorted into exams, plans and to-dos.

Upload exported WhatsApp chats. Every message gets filed by what it is about: exams, study material, things you scheduled, tasks, payments, links, files and places. Dates mentioned in messages (“Thursday at 18:00”, “מחר בשעה 10”, “12/12”) land on a timeline.

Sorting runs in your browser. Messages only leave it when you press “Sort with AI”.
Drop .txt or .zip files here
How to export a chat from WhatsApp
  1. Android: open the chat → ⋮ menu → More → Export chat → Without media.
  2. iPhone: open the chat → tap the chat name → Export Chat → Without Media.
  3. Save the file (Files, Drive or email it to yourself), then pick it here. You can add several chats at once.
